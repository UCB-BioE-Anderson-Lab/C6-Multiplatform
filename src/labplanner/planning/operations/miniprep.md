# Miniprep

Straightforward except for one thing, and that one thing is not mechanical.

*"this is straightforward, but need to specify and reserve a place to put the samples in an
existing box or a new box. You have the llm do this, and make calls on the inventory to find a
good spot."* — JCA, 2026-09-10

## Reserving the space is the work

Every miniprep produces a tube that has to live somewhere, and the labsheet has to say **which
box and which well** before anybody is holding it. That means querying the inventory for what is
already there and choosing — an existing box with room near related samples, or a new box.

**"A good spot" is judgement.** Adjacent wells for samples that will be used together, a whole
row or a whole column for a set, a new box rather than scattering a series across the gaps in an
old one. The inventory says what is free; it does not say what is sensible.

**Reserve it, do not just suggest it.** Two labsheets written the same afternoon that both pick
"the next free well" collide, and the collision is discovered by someone standing at the −20 with
a tube in their hand.

## No checkpoint

*"Miniprep has no checkpoint. Samples just get logged on the sheet. When the full experiment is
over, they send you back that sheet, so you can update the inventory with the new samples at the
end."*

The rows the student fills in here are what the inventory is updated from — which is why the
packet ends with the whole workbook coming back (`return.workbook`), and why asking separately
for miniprep results would be asking twice for one fact.
