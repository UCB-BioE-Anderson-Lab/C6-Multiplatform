# Gel

*"You need the expected sizes from the C6 simulation, and that's it."* — JCA, 2026-09-10

One lane per PCR product in the bin, each labelled with the size the simulation says to expect.
That number is the entire point of the step: a gel with no expected size tells you a band exists,
not whether it is the right band.

`c6-sim` gives it. Where a product cannot be simulated, **say so on the sheet** rather than
leaving the column blank — a blank reads as "no band expected", and the two cases are opposite.
(`SLIP5/SIMULATION-NOTES.md` has a live example: pGhost17's PCRs fail C6's 18 nt annealing rule
because of a designed mutation, and are perfectly good reactions.)

## There is always a checkpoint

*"There will always be a gel pic checkpoint with these steps."* `checkpoint.gel` — a photo, the
lane labels, and the student's reading of it. Not conditional on anything.
