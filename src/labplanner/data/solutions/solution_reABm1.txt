>solution	reABm1
>description	"Recycled A+B medium. Part B is spent cells lysed with base; the acidic part A neutralizes it on mixing. 1% sucrose final."
>volume	0.5
>source	Pimar ABm5.xlsx, sheet "reAB1"

>>composition	amt	units	note
part A	250	mL	PREMIXED AND ACIDIC -- everything but the base
sucrose	5	g	1% w/v final. NOT 5% like ABm6 -- see the procedure
2M MgSO4	2.5	mL	10 mM final
1M CaCl2	333	uL	0.67 mM final
trace_metal_soln	333	uL	the "trace salts" line; filter sterilized, added after cooling
0.5 M H3PO4	20	mL	the acid half of the neutralization; triprotic, ~2 protons each to neutral
0.5 M NH4Cl	20	mL	the nitrogen source; M9's phosphate and ammonium are not added, the lysate carries them
part B	250	mL	THE LYSATE -- made from twice its own volume of culture, so the cells are 2x concentrated
saturated culture	500	mL	spun down; the pellet is the input, the spent supernatant is discarded
water	10	mL	to resuspend the pellet before lysis
0.5 M NaOH	30	mL	the bulk of the base that lyses the cells
0.5 M KOH	10	mL	the rest of the base; replaces the KCl the earlier Lys100 formulation needed

>>procedure
Lyse the cells with base to make part B, then mix that with the premixed acidic part A. The
mixing IS the neutralization: 20 mmol of OH- in part B against 10 mmol of triprotic H3PO4 in
part A, which gives up about two protons each on the way to neutral. That is why part A is
premixed acidic and why neither half is usable alone.

1. Spin down the saturated culture and discard the spent supernatant. The BIOMASS is what is
   recycled here, not the conditioned medium.
2. Resuspend the pellet in the water.
3. Add the NaOH and the KOH. Hold warm until the suspension goes transparent -- in the original
   screen, NaOH was the only agent of about twelve tried that reached transparency, and
   transparency is the endpoint rather than a fixed time.
4. Bring part B to volume.
5. Mix equal parts A and B. Add antibiotic.

MEASURE THE pH OF THE FINISHED MEDIUM AND WRITE IT DOWN. It has never been measured. The
recycling record asks for it in its own words -- "I didn't check any of the pH's", "probably
more like pH 6 based on litmus", "we need to control pH better on the next runs". There is no
pass band here because nobody has established one; the reading is the point.

THE SUCROSE IS 1%, WHERE ABm6 IS 5%. That is the formulation as written, and it means an ABm6
arm and a reABm1 arm differ in TWO things: where the salts came from, and five-fold in carbon.
An experiment comparing them is not comparing recycling alone unless one of those is matched.

SCALING. The unit above is 500 mL of medium from 500 mL of culture. For 2 L of medium from 2 L
of culture, take everything x4: part A gets 20 g sucrose, 10 mL 2M MgSO4, 1.33 mL each of CaCl2
and trace_metal_soln, and 80 mL each of 0.5 M H3PO4 and 0.5 M NH4Cl; part B takes the whole 2 L
of culture, 40 mL water, 120 mL 0.5 M NaOH and 40 mL 0.5 M KOH.

PROVENANCE OF THE FORMULATION: it descends from Lys100, which used NaOH alone with NH4Cl and
KCl added back and the phosphate separate. The sheet's own note: "It may become necessary to put
in the phosphate separate from the others as done with Lys100, but worth trying the simplest
first." If the premixed part A precipitates, that is the fallback.
