>solution	ABm7
>description	"ABm6's part A with double-strength salts, mixed 2:1:1 to leave room for supplements."
>volume	0.5
>source	Pimar ABm5.xlsx, sheet "ABm7"

>>composition	amt	units	note
part A	250	mL	identical to ABm6 part A; this is half the 500 mL unit, but mixed 2:1:1
sucrose	25	g
2M MgSO4	2.5	mL
1M CaCl2	333	uL
trace_metal_soln	333	uL
part B	250	mL
M9 salts	12	g	double ABm6's, because part B is one quarter of the final volume

>>procedure
Mix 2 parts A, 1 part B, and 1 part water or supplements.

The point of the formulation is the fourth quarter: it is left open so a supplement can go in
without diluting the salts or the carbon source below target. Sterilize the parts separately as
for ABm6.
