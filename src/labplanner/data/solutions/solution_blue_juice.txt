>solution	blue juice
>description	"Gel loading buffer without the stain: 1x TAE, 10% glycerol, bromophenol blue and xylene cyanol. LOAD is made from it (100 uL blue juice + 1 uL 10% DNA dye)."
>volume	0.05
>source	JCA, 2026-10-28, by email: "basically TAE with glycerol at 10% ish and blue dye which is a mix of bb and xc", with 100 uL of the bb/xc mix per batch

>>composition	amt	units	note
glycerol	5	mL	100%, straight from the bottle. 10% v/v final. Viscous: weigh 6.3 g instead of pipetting
50x TAE	1	mL	1x final
bb/xc dye mix	100	uL	the bromophenol blue / xylene cyanol mix already made up in the lab
water	to 50 mL	mL	brings it to volume

>>procedure
1. Weigh 6.3 g glycerol (5 mL) into a 50 mL conical. Weighing is faster and more accurate than pipetting it.
2. Add 1 mL 50x TAE and 100 uL bb/xc dye mix.
3. Bring to 50 mL with water, cap, and invert until no glycerol streaks remain.
4. Label BLUE JUICE with the date and your initials. Store at room temperature; it keeps.

THERE IS NO DNA STAIN IN THIS. Blue juice keeps for months; the stain does not, so it goes in
only when LOAD is made, 100 uL at a time. See protocol preparation_of_gel_loading_dye.
