>solution	ABm8
>description	"ABm6 with agar -- the plate version."
>volume	0.5
>source	Pimar ABm5.xlsx, sheet "ABm8"

>>composition	amt	units	note
part A	250	mL	identical to ABm6 part A
sucrose	25	g
2M MgSO4	2.5	mL
1M CaCl2	333	uL
trace_metal_soln	333	uL
part B	250	mL
M9 salts	6	g
agar	3.75	g	1.5% w/v in part B, 0.75% in the poured plate

>>procedure
Mix equal parts of A and B.

The agar goes in part B, which is the half without sucrose -- so part B is the one that must be
melted and held hot, and part A can be added after it cools enough not to caramelize. Sterilize
separately as for ABm6.
