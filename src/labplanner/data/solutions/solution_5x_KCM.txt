>solution	5x KCM
>description	"Potassium-calcium-magnesium salts, 5x. Added to a thawed aliquot of TSS-competent cells just before the DNA, to 1x final."
>volume	0.1
>source	openwetware.org/wiki/IGEM:UC_Berkeley/2006/Plasmid_Transformation, which gives 0.5 M KCl / 0.15 M CaCl2 / 0.25 M MgCl2

>>composition	amt	units	note
KCl	3.73	g	0.5 M
CaCl2.2H2O	2.21	g	0.15 M. Anhydrous instead: 1.66 g
MgCl2.6H2O	5.08	g	0.25 M. Anhydrous instead: 2.38 g
water	to 100 mL	mL	brings it to volume

>>procedure
1. Dissolve in most of the water, bring to 100 mL.
2. Autoclave.
3. Store at 4 degC.

USED AT 1x, AND THE DILUTION DEPENDS ON THE PROTOCOL. `heat_shock_transformation.js` adds 25 uL of
this 5x stock to a 100 uL cell aliquot, which is 1x in 125 uL. The Berkeley page it comes from
instead builds a 100 uL reaction from 20 uL cells, 10 uL of 5x, 70 uL water and 1 uL DNA, and uses
a larger 15 uL into a 150 uL reaction for ligation mixes and cotransformations. All are 1x final;
the difference is how much cell suspension goes in.
