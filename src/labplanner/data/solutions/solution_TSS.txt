>solution	TSS
>description	"Transformation and Storage Solution. LB with PEG, DMSO and magnesium; cells are resuspended in it at ~5% of culture volume and frozen competent in the same solution."
>volume	1
>source	Chung, Niemela & Miller, PNAS 1989 86:2172-2175; recipe as given at openwetware.org/wiki/TSS, scaled x20 from its 50 mL

>>composition	amt	units	note
PEG 8000	100	g	10% w/v. The paper reports PEG 3350 as more efficient; other sizes cost a little efficiency
1 M MgCl2	30	mL	30 mM final. As the salt instead: 6.1 g MgCl2.6H2O
DMSO	50	mL	5% v/v
LB	to 1 L	mL	brings it to volume

>>procedure
WHY THIS FILE EXISTS. `protocols/modules/tss_comp_cells.js` tells you to resuspend the pellet in
ice-cold TSS and never says what TSS is. Anyone following that module had to go and find this.

1. Dissolve the PEG in most of the LB. It is slow; warm it if needed.
2. Add the MgCl2.
3. ADJUST TO pH 6.5 BEFORE STERILISING. The paper's TSS is deliberately acidic.
4. Bring to volume with LB.
5. Filter sterilise, 0.22 um. DO NOT AUTOCLAVE -- it precipitates.
6. ADD THE DMSO AFTER FILTERING unless the filter is DMSO-resistant. Cellulose nitrate is not.
7. Store at 4 degC or -20 degC.

THE MAGNESIUM IS THE ONE NUMBER THAT DISAGREES. The paper's abstract says 50 mM Mg2+; the
OpenWetWare recipe scaled here gives 30 mM (1.5 mL of 1 M into 50 mL). Other published versions
use 20-50 mM, and some split it between MgCl2 and MgSO4. 30 mM is what this file makes because
that is the recipe it came from; if you want the paper's number it is 50 mL of 1 M per litre,
or 10.2 g of the hexahydrate. Decide once and write down which you used.

HOW MUCH YOU NEED. The competent-cell module resuspends at about 5% of culture volume, so a litre
of TSS serves roughly 20 L of culture and makes on the order of ten thousand 100 uL aliquots.
