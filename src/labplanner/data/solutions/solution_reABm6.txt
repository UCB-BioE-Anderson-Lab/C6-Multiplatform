>>procedure
THE NAME IS NEW; THE FORMULATION IS NOT. The recycling runs used this and never named it -- the
spreadsheet sheet is called `recycled1` and the issue calls the runs recycling1 to recycling4.
Called reABm6 here because that is what it is: ABm6 with the M9-salt half replaced by lysate.
Coined in C6 on 2026-10-21 so an experiment can name the thing it is making.

1. Spin down the saturated culture and discard the spent supernatant. The BIOMASS is recycled,
   not the conditioned medium.
2. Resuspend the pellet in the water.
3. Add the NaOH. Hold warm until the suspension goes transparent -- transparency is the
   endpoint, not a time. NaOH was the only agent of about twelve screened that reached it.
4. Neutralize with the H3PO4, then add the NH4Cl and the KCl.
5. Bring part B to volume, then mix equal parts A and B. Add antibiotic.

WHY THIS AND NOT reABm1. They are the same idea arranged differently, and the difference that
matters to an experiment is the sugar: this one takes ABm6's part A unchanged, so it is 5%
sucrose and matches ABm6; reABm1 drops to 1% and premixes the acid into part A instead. An ABm6
arm against a reABm1 arm differs in two things at once, the salt source and five-fold in carbon.
An ABm6 arm against a reABm6 arm differs in one. JCA, 2026-10-21: "Let's use the formulation
that is discussed in the issues for the earlier gfp experiments."

MEASURE THE pH AND WRITE IT DOWN. It has never been measured in any run of this -- "I didn't
check any of the pH's", "probably more like pH 6 based on litmus", "we need to control pH better
on the next runs". There is no pass band because nobody has established one; the reading is the
point, and two runs of this medium with recorded pH would establish one.

SCALING IS BY CULTURE VOLUME. The unit here is 50 mL of medium from 50 mL of culture: the cells
end up 2x concentrated, because 50 mL of culture makes only 25 mL of part B. So the culture
volume you need equals the medium volume you want.
