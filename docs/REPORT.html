  • s2-dilution: where the 1 unlocated oligo stock(s) are — the sheet asks, and the returned workbook is what updates the inventory
  • s6-pick: selection criteria — what counts as a colony worth picking here
  • s7-miniprep-sequencing: which box these minipreps go into — `box=` on the Miniprep line settles it. The well is written at the −20 and comes back on the sheet.
  • s7-miniprep-sequencing: what these clones are clones of — nothing declared it, so they are named after the transformation’s product. Say `clone=<name>` on a Miniprep line in the characterization file to settle it.
  • s7-miniprep-sequencing: which oligo to sequence with, and whether this is a region or the whole plasmid — c6-sim <cf> --primes <oligo> says where an oligo sits and whether it has more than one site
